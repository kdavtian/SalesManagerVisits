# Business and finance logic encoded in the workbook

## 1. The business in one paragraph
KAD buys finished lubricants in USD from four suppliers (GT Group and Sole Trans -> Castrol; Luk Oil -> Royal; Orlen Oil -> Lotos/Orlen), clears them through customs, stores them in its own warehouse and sells them in AMD through channels: field **sales managers by territory** (SM YVN = Yerevan, SM Shirak, SM Davtashen, SM CAS, SM B2B) and **key/institutional channels** (KF key accounts/distributors, CAS Castrol-service garages, OEM dealers, PCO oil-change points, CVO commercial vehicles/fleets). Cash comes in as customer payments (`Cashflow For="Oil order"`) and goes out for purchases, customs, tax, transport, payroll and overheads. The owner funds peaks with a bank credit line (Evoca) and USD exchanges. Real sales history starts 2025-01 with a few large aggregated invoices per month (Jan-Apr 2025) and becomes line-by-line per customer from 2025-05; the 2024 ORDERS rows (and some 2025 ones) are only the internal "Inventory" account's monthly stock-out entries with Total 0.

## 2. Cost model (Products sheet) - how landing cost and net cost are produced
Per unit (pack): `Cost USD = Pack Cost USD / Items per Pack`.
- Transportation Legal (USD): Castrol = Netto x 2007/9280 (a 2007-USD truck over 9280 kg); Lotos = Netto x 0.35; others = Netto x O1 (0.35). Netto = kg per unit.
- Transport Real = 0.252 x Netto (USD; actual freight).
- Ecology 2% and Customs 5% of (Cost + Transportation Legal); Excise = Netto x 682 AMD / USD-rate (per kg); VAT 20% of (Cost + Transportation Legal + Customs + Excise); Customs Costs = 0.01664 USD/L (fixed clearance cost (88k+44k+25k AMD per ~25,000 L)).
- **Landing Cost USD** = sum of all of the above (+ Other Costs); **Landing Cost AMD** = x 377.5 (Products!A1; the Purchase_IN sheet uses 385 - purchase value and landed value are not on the same rate).
- **Net Cost AMD** = Landing Cost + 4% x Bronze price (provision for manager salary/commission) + profit tax 18% x (Bronze - Landing + real transport in AMD). So Net Cost is "landing cost fully loaded with variable selling cost and profit tax", computed **on the bronze price**. For products with no bronze price both add-ons are 0, so their Net Cost = Landing Cost (understated). Use landing cost for COGS/inventory value, net cost for "after commissions and tax" profitability.

## 3. Price lists (4 columns, typed in Products, mirrored in PriceList)
`Price T1 Bronze` (standard/wholesale for ordinary accounts), `Price T2 Silver` (better price), `Price T3 Gold` (best, key accounts), `Retail Price` (shelf price for end customers). Typical Edge Professional EC 0w20 1L: Bronze 9,700 / Silver 8,700 / Gold 6,000 / Retail 12,000 / Landing 3,862 / Net 5,315. Typical ratios across the catalogue: silver = 0.875 x bronze, gold = 0.88 x silver, gold = ~1.43 x landing cost (12 of 163 gold prices are BELOW landing cost - a check worth running on every new price list). In 2.0.7 the owner added the Tier on Customers (Gold/Silver) so the price list can be chosen per customer; PriceList `Discount` = (Retail - Bronze)/Retail shows how far below shelf price the standard price sits (median ~17%).
Reality check from ORDERS: the UnitPrice typed on lines equals an official tier price only ~44% of the time (gold 40%, silver 6%, bronze 1%); effective price (UnitPrice+Discount) averages ~88% of silver. i.e. most selling prices are negotiated; the tier price lists are guidance, discounts are recorded per unit in `Discount`.
Business rules agreed with the owner: bronze empty -> use silver; gold customers can have individually negotiated prices (app: `customer_product_prices`); the app's customer tier follows the workbook's `Tier` column on every sync; warehouse/products pages show the **silver** price as the default wholesale price.

## 4. Sales (ORDERS)
Revenue = sum of `Total` = Qty x (UnitPrice + Discount). Liters = Size x Qty. Returns are negative Qty rows (57 rows, ~-2.7M AMD) - net into sales. Gifts/campaign lines have Discount = -UnitPrice (Total 0). Payment method is a property of the order: **Cash** (settled at delivery; cash goes to a rep's cash box) vs **Invoice** (bank transfer; the accountant issues a tax invoice). Order IDs are date-coded, so `Date` can be recomputed from the ID. Channel (Sales_Rep) drives plans, KPI bonuses, performance pages.

## 5. Collections, receivables, aging
- Payments = Cashflow rows `For="Oil order"` keyed by Customer ID (not name). `Debt(c) = Balance0(c) + SUM(ORDERS.Total) - SUM(Cashflow payments)`. As-of-date debt uses `<= date` on both sums (that is exactly what the app implements with `balance0_amd`, `order_lines`, `cashflow_lines`).
- `Balance0` = opening debt/credit before the ledger started (a few big accounts carry millions; Balance0-only customers have debt without orders).
- Aging in the bot/app: days since last payment (`Last Payment`), buckets 0-30 / 31-60 / 61-90 / 91-180 / 180+ (and "never paid"). The workbook's own `Risk` label (High/Medium/Low/No Debt) is a rule-of-thumb (see sheet-dictionary) and can contradict the bot's aging (e.g. "No Debt" with Debit > 0 is counted by the bot as a contradiction).
- Cash discipline KPI: Collected / Sales (collection ratio). 2026 YTD it is ~91% (collections 229.8M vs sales 251.6M by 2026-10-03). Debt as of 2026-10-03 = 101.8M, of which about 66M is "paid within 30 days" (active customers) and ~26.5M is 180+ days or never paid (24.1M of it belongs to 24 customers who have never paid anything, mostly Balance0-only accounts).

## 6. Cash ledger and company liquidity
Every movement in/out of every pot is a row in `Cash` (signed). Pots (`Source`): physical cash boxes (Cash, per-rep boxes YVN/DVT/Shirak/Gevorg/Slav), bank accounts (EvocaBank AMD/USD, ABB Bank, ArmSwiss), `Evoca Credit Line` (a borrowing facility - positive when drawn), `Varki Gumar` (a separate fund, zero now). `For` categories split into: customer receipts (Oil order); **cost of goods** (Purchase = supplier payments, Customs, Tax = import taxes/VAT, Transportation); **operating expenses** (Salary, Office, Fuel, Maintenance, Marketing, PPE, Interest, Other); **financing/equity** (Loan, Credit Line, Investment, Divident, Personal, Armen Loans); **internal** (To bank, Transfer, Exchange, Balance - net-zero pairs or reclassifications; `Balance` rows are balancing corrections of opening balances). Control panel formula `B1` = AMD cash + bank pots + USD pots x `L1` (380) = approximate liquid money (cached with stale month `A1`). Operating cash cost level: salaries ~3.2M/month, marketing ~1M, fuel ~0.3M, office 0.6-2.7M, dividends 1M/month (2026).
Import economics from the ledger: 2025-2026 YTD cash paid for Purchase 137M + Customs 129M + Tax 48M + Transportation 11M AMD while the landing-cost model puts landed USD cost at a median ~1.9x the supplier USD price for Castrol (2.3x Royal, 2.9x Lotos) because customs, excise and **import VAT (20%, normally creditable) are included in Landing Cost** - confirm with the owner/accountant whether VAT should be inside cost, and use the ledger payments to reconcile the model against reality.

## 7. Inventory and stock-days
`QTY = OB + purchases - ORDERS.Qty` (customer 10000 movements reduce stock too, so internal transfers/write-offs are visible). Value at landing cost (182.3M AMD, 80.6k liters on 2026-10-03; at silver price 298.7M). Velocity = all-time average per month since the first purchase of that product; the Days column is therefore a rough cover. The app and bot compute a better **recent** cover: demand over 30/90 days; thresholds agreed with the owner: < 45 days critical, 45-90 low, >= 90 ok; dead stock = stock with no sales; subtotal per product in pcs and liters.

## 8. Profitability benchmarks (derived 2026-10-03, landing cost of goods sold at *current* landing cost)
Gross margin ~33.5% (net-cost margin ~18.5%). By brand: Castrol 35% (net 20%), Lotos 30% (15%), Royal 27% (11%), Orlen 23%. By channel (landing GM): OEM 39%, KF 37%, SM B2B 41%, SM YVN 34%, SM Shirak 34%, PCO 30%, SM CAS 29%, CVO 27%, CAS 25% (net-cost margin only 2.5%: thin). Average realised price per liter: Castrol ~4,640, Lotos ~2,400, Royal ~2,700 AMD/L. Use these as sanity ranges when checking a report; a margin far outside them usually means a missing cost (empty PriceList landing cost), a wrong ProductID match, or a unit-price typo.

## 9. Management layers
- **Compensation model (Sales Team_2026)**: each channel's bonus pool = 2% of collected cash; split into KPI1 (liters vs plan per brand: Castrol 45%, Lotos 30%, Royal 25%), KPI2 (collection ratio), KPI3 (new customers). Payout multipliers by attainment (e.g. >120% -> x1.3, >100% -> x1.15, >90% -> x1.0, >80% -> x0.9, else 0). Fixed base: Sales Director 400k, managers 180k (+ own rules per rep). The bot's `sales_performance` payload reads Sales/Collected/Budget (Budget = the 2% pool, not a sales target!). **The app's "Plan" is a separate AMD sales plan** set in the app (Team Performance); don't confuse it with `Plan_2026` (liters) or `Budget` (bonus pool).
- **Payroll (Tabel)**: monthly Amount = fixed + 2% of the position's collected revenue (+30k per new client for the CAS manager), paid partly via bank (`Fix bank`) and partly cash, reconciled by `Info` text = "<Position> <YYYY> <MON>" in Cash. Sensitive.
- **Plan_2026**: liters per channel x brand per month; actual from ORDERS; KPI% = actual/plan (feeds KPI1).

## 10. How the pieces reach the app/bot
ORDERS -> app `erp_order_lines` (sales page, debt as-of, discounts shown under lines); Cashflow Oil-order -> `erp_cashflow_lines`; Debits+Customers -> `erp_customer_data` (debt, balance0, last payment, aging, region/subregion, tier); PriceList/Products -> `products` (tier prices, landing/net cost, hc_code, stock, family); Sales Team -> `sales_performance`; ORDERS liters by brand/channel/month -> `brand_volume`; Inventory + velocity -> stock-days. Reports (CEO daily/weekly/monthly, sales-director, debt Excel) are generated by the bot from the same data.
