## Topic map (domains)
Core: D1 customer master · D2 product master & prices · D3 sales documents (order → posted sale) · D4 returns / credit notes · D5 customer receipts & cash custody · D6 company cash ledger (all Cashflow categories, bank) · D7 customer debt / AR ledger · D8 inventory ledger · D9 purchasing / imports / landed cost / supplier payables · D10 plan & KPI actuals · D11 payroll calculation & salary payments · D12 reports, dashboards, Vaghinak, KAD OS, Platform (consumers) · D13 the CEO bot (Telegram reports).
Adjacent (beginners miss): D14 backups & disaster recovery · D15 posting controls (immutability, numbering, period lock, audit trail, segregation of duties) · D16 opening balances & data migration/cleansing · D17 reconciliation engine & per-module source switch · D18 accounting-program feed (books stay outside) · D19 Armenian tax-document rules (e-settlement documents, cash register, retention, excise) · D20 personal-data protection · D21 people/change (accountant's daily routine moves from Excel to app).
